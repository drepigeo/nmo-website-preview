Uniting Doctors & Medicos in the Mission of National Healthcare Service

स्वास्थ्य सेवा, राष्ट्र सेवा — Swasthya Sewa, Rashtra Sewa

The National Medicos Organisation (NMO) is a nationwide fraternity of qualified allopathic doctors, dental surgeons, faculties, and medical students dedicated to serving the underprivileged, tribal, and remote frontiers of Bharat with compassionate clinical care and national reconstruction.

Origin & Ethos

Cultivating Compassion & Nationalism Within Healthcare

Founded on 8th August 1977 and registered under the Societies Registration Act (Reg No. 21/1987-88), NMO bridges modern medical science with patriotic, humanitarian service.

Conceived at Varanasi by medical student Dr. Dhanakar Thakur along with over 40 pioneering medicos and senior faculty, NMO was established as a democratic, national platform of allopathic and dental doctors and students committed to positive national health irrespective of caste, creed, or background.

Beyond community healthcare, NMO is committed to re-orienting medical education toward national reconstruction, enhancing teacher-student mentorship in medical colleges, and advocating for progressive healthcare policies that honor indigenous cultural ethos.

Swasthya Sewa

Bringing clinical excellence to remote, border, and tribal populations.

Rashtra Sewa

Inculcating discipline, character, and selflessness among medicos.
